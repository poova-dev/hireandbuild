The 6 Pillars That Power Every Replica Build

How we have maintained a 100% on-time completion record and zero legal disputes across 400+ homes in Chennai.

1

Guaranteed Delivery Timelines

Every turnkey agreement has a legally agreed calendar schedule. In the rare event of unapproved delay, we commit to weekly compensation penalties.

2

Fixed Cost BOQ Guarantee

Once your contract is executed, the price per square foot is locked. Steel, cement, or sand price hikes during construction are absorbed by us.
